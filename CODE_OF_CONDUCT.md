# Code of conduct

Clerk-san contributors must keep project participation respectful, safe, and focused on improving the
software. This policy applies to pull requests, commits, reviews, project discussions, and other spaces
where someone represents the project.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code without attacking the person who proposed them.
- Give specific, evidence-based feedback and accept correction in good faith.
- Respect different backgrounds, identities, experience levels, languages, and accessibility needs.
- Protect private information and use only fictional, synthetic documents in project materials.
- Follow the project's local-first, human-review, security, and truthful-claims boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, sexualized attention, threats, intimidation, or sustained disruption.
- Insults, personal attacks, doxxing, stalking, or publishing another person's private information.
- Uploading or linking real receipts, invoices, personal documents, customer data, credentials, logs,
  databases, or private model input/output.
- Publicly disclosing a vulnerability instead of following [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- Knowingly making unsupported claims of production readiness, legal certification, regulatory
  compliance, or security assurance on behalf of the project.

## Moderation and reporting

The maintainer may edit or remove project content, reject contributions, restrict participation, or
ban an account when conduct is unsafe or persistently disruptive. Responses should be proportionate to
the conduct, its impact, and whether the participant corrects the behavior.

Use GitHub's platform reporting and blocking tools for abuse. A repository-visible violation that does
not contain sensitive information may also be brought to the maintainer's attention in the affected
pull request. Do not repeat private material or publish sensitive allegations to request moderation. A
separate private conduct mailbox is not currently advertised. Security vulnerabilities must use the
private route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not a conduct report.

Retaliation against a person who reports a concern in good faith is prohibited. Deliberately false or
malicious reports may themselves result in moderation.
